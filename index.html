Typing Tower Defense

Click inside the game once so it picks up your keyboard. For a bigger screen, open it in its own tab.

Hordes are coming for your tower. Your words are the only weapon you have.

About

Typing Tower Defense is an educational typing game. You are a ranged fighter standing on top of a tower while hordes of enemies march toward you. Each enemy carries a word. Type it correctly and you knock the enemy back. Let them reach the base and your tower loses health.

Early enemies need one short word. As the waves grow, enemies need more words and harder ones, including contractions. Every wave ends with a boss.

Gameplay

1. Pick a difficulty

Start the game and choose a difficulty. Each level is made of several waves of enemies

2. Type to survive

Enemies are fed words in order. A correct word costs the enemy health. A wrong word does nothing, so fix it or switch to another enemy

3. Buff up

Between waves you pick a buff for the next one

4. Beat the boss

Every wave ends with a boss that has to be defeated before the wave counts as cleared

5. Unlock New Weapons

Defeated enemies add points to your score. Browse the shop and spend on cosmetics, and check the high scores screen from the main menu
